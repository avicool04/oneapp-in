Privacy Policy — OneApp.in

Last updated: 26 September 2026

OneApp ("we", "us") respects your privacy and protects your personal data in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address (or basic Google profile) when you sign up.
  • Documents you choose to upload (IDs, policies, bills) and their metadata (type, expiry dates).
  • Guardian contacts you explicitly add for emergency access.

2. How we use it

  • To store and show your documents securely in your personal vault.
  • To send expiry and renewal reminders you have enabled.
  • To grant emergency access ONLY to guardians you approve, under verification rules you set.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never scan your documents for advertising.
  • We never share documents with third parties except where legally required.

4. Security

Files are stored in encrypted cloud storage with per-user access controls. Emergency access releases are time-limited, watermarked and logged for audit.

5. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

You may view, correct, download, or permanently delete your data at any time from your account.
